# Storefront Design Notes

## What changed

- Refined the palette around Yale blue with maroon, coral, warm cream, and sky-blue accents so the storefront feels distinctive and energetic.
- Added smoother hover, reveal, and image transitions across product cards, buttons, links, and the chat launcher.
- Strengthened visual hierarchy with editorial serif headlines, compact mono labels, bold price treatment, and clearer collection controls.
- Gave the chat a relaxed, friendly personality through conversational copy, softer bubbles, quick-start prompts, and a warmer maroon header.
- Added subtle product-card lift, image zoom, and color-blocked collection sections to make browsing feel tactile and memorable.
- Added reduced-motion handling so the experience stays comfortable for shoppers who prefer less animation.

## Why it helps

The stronger color system and hierarchy make the site feel like a real Yale storefront instead of a generic catalogue. Smooth motion gives product interactions a premium feel without slowing the shopper down. The friendlier chat presentation lowers the effort to ask for help, while more expressive product cards make browsing inviting and encourage shoppers to continue into product details.
